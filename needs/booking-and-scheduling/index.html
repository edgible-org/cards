Booking and Scheduling

Self-hosted alternatives to Calendly, Doodle and Google Calendar booking pages.

What people say

  • members book our courts or rooms
  • schedule games or matches
  • arrange a time that suits everyone
  • let customers book appointments with us

Why people self-host it

People leave court booking apps, Calendly, Doodle, Google Calendar booking pages for it. Why, strongest first: cost, control.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Fits, with conditions works well, but a club or community depends on it: it needs a machine that stays on, usually a small cloud server rather than a volunteer's computer, and someone besides one volunteer to look after it

Questions that decide it

  • who looks after it when you step down?
  • a home machine, or a small cloud server at a few dollars a month? and do bookings need payment?

How to publish it

Usually
none with the app's own member accounts, because members and customers are not in your Edgible organization; sign-up closed or approved by an admin
When the data is private or sensitive
the same; members' contact details stay in the app

Who sees what inside it

admins set booking rules, such as length, how far ahead, and who may book which resource

The machine

always on, so usually a small cloud server; little else

Cards for it

No card yet. Apps for it are listed on awesome-selfhosted. An agent with the edgible-cards skill can shortlist apps and build one, or you can ask for one.
