Internet of Things (IoT)

Self-hosted alternatives to SmartThings, Google Home and Alexa routines.

What people say

  • home automation
  • control my smart home from anywhere
  • one app for all my smart devices
  • keep my smart home working without the cloud

Why people self-host it

People leave SmartThings, Google Home, Alexa routines, vendor cloud apps for it. Why, strongest first: it has to run at home, privacy, control, a service that shut down or got worse.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Fits, with conditions it must run on a machine at home, on the same network as the devices; reaching it from outside without opening router ports is where Edgible helps

Questions that decide it

  • which devices or brands do you have?
  • control from outside the home, or only at home?

How to publish it

Usually
org for the web interface; the companion phone apps cannot pass an Edgible sign-in, so they need the app's own login on a hostname without one
When the data is private or sensitive
org only, and the phone apps at home

The machine

always on, at home, on the devices' network (host networking for discovery)
