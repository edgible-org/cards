Media Streaming - Multimedia Streaming

Self-hosted alternatives to Plex Pass.

What people say

  • stream my films and music to my TV and phone
  • my own Netflix for home videos
  • share my media library with family

Why people self-host it

People leave Plex Pass, Netflix for owned media, iTunes Match for it. Why, strongest first: control, cost.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Good fit the web interface works through Edgible; native TV and phone apps may not pass an Edgible sign-in

Questions that decide it

  • which devices do you watch on?

How to publish it

Usually
org for the web (the jellyfin card); native apps on the home network
When the data is private or sensitive
org, or home network only

The machine

disk for the library; transcoding is heavy on CPU, a GPU helps
