Content Management Systems (CMS)

Self-hosted alternatives to Squarespace, Wix and WordPress.com.

What people say

  • cheaper hosting for my business website
  • edit my website without a developer
  • a website with members or restricted content
  • move off Squarespace or Wix

Why people self-host it

People leave Squarespace, Wix, WordPress.com, managed WordPress hosting for it. Why, strongest first: cost, control.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Fits, with conditions good for a CMS on a machine that stays on, usually a small cloud server; a static site is better on free static hosting; a site that earns money needs someone to keep it up

Questions that decide it

  • is the site static, or edited in a CMS?
  • does it have members or paid content?

How to publish it

Usually
the site on none; its editor or admin on org where the app allows a separate hostname
When the data is private or sensitive
n/a, a website is public

The machine

always on, with a reliable connection, so usually a small cloud server
