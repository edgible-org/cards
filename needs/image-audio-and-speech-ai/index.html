Image, audio, and speech AI

Self-hosted alternatives to Midjourney, Otter.ai, ElevenLabs and Descript transcription.

What people say

  • generate images on my own GPU
  • transcribe meetings privately
  • text to speech without a subscription
  • use my gaming PC's GPU from my laptop
  • run Stable Diffusion or ComfyUI from anywhere

Why people self-host it

People leave Midjourney, Otter.ai, ElevenLabs, Descript transcription for it. Why, strongest first: cost, privacy, control.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Fits, with conditions needs a GPU or Apple Silicon; it fits well when the person already has one at home

Questions that decide it

  • what GPU or Mac do you have?
  • images, transcription, or voice?

How to publish it

Usually
org: tools such as ComfyUI have no login of their own, so never none; an API for other apps on api-key
When the data is private or sensitive
org only, or not published

The machine

a GPU with plenty of memory, or Apple Silicon; lots of disk for models
