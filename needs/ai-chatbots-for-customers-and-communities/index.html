AI chatbots for customers and communities

Self-hosted alternatives to Intercom Fin, Chatbase, Tidio, Botpress Cloud and Voiceflow.

What people say

  • a chatbot for my website
  • an AI that answers customer questions from our docs
  • a support bot
  • an AI bot for our Discord, Slack, or Telegram

Why people self-host it

People leave Intercom Fin, Chatbase, Tidio, Botpress Cloud, Voiceflow for it. Why, strongest first: cost, control, privacy.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Fits, with conditions the public talks to it, so it must stay up and its model costs must be capped; a hosted chatbot is often simpler for a small business

Questions that decide it

  • who will talk to it: customers, members, or your team?
  • which model: a hosted API with a spending limit, or a local one?

How to publish it

Usually
the chat widget or bot webhook on none, because the public and chat platforms cannot sign in; the admin and knowledge base on org; set a spending limit on any paid model API, and rate limits in the app
When the data is private or sensitive
the same, with only public information in its knowledge base: anything the bot can read, a visitor can get it to say
When the data is regulated
do not let it answer from client, patient, or financial records; send those questions to a person

Who sees what inside it

a public bot answers anyone, so its knowledge base must hold only what anyone may see

The machine

always on, so usually a small cloud server; a GPU only for a local model
