Add your app

You built an app, maybe with an AI. Add it to the cards and anyone can run it with one prompt, on their own machine, in the same tested way. You keep your app, your licence and your repository: a card only describes how to run it.

The steps, with what to say to your AI, are in the Edgible for Contributors playbook:

  1. Make your app a card: your AI checks your app, tells you what a card needs, and builds what's missing.
  2. Test it and share it: a test on your own Edgible, a pull request, and a listing with who looks after it.

Stuck, or not sure it's ready? Open an issue (opens in a new tab), or write to us on the contact page (opens in a new tab).
