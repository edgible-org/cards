AI Playbooks

Each playbook takes you from nothing to something working, one short chapter at a time. Your AI assistant does the technical parts: you mostly tell it what you want.

The playbooks use Edgible cards: ready-made, tested apps that your assistant sets up for you. An app you built yourself can still be published with Edgible, but the steps here were written and tested for cards.

Pick a path. Most start with Get Edgible running.

Edgible for AI

Your own agent and a private model, used from your phone, on a budget.

Twelve chapters in two playbooks →

Edgible for Operations

Back it up, move it, upgrade it, and know when something's wrong, so it keeps running.

Three chapters so far, more coming →

Edgible for Contributors

Built an app? Turn it into a card, test it on your own Edgible, and share it so anyone can run it.

Two chapters →

Edgible for OpenCode

An open-source coding agent in a box you can reset in seconds, on a budget you set, with or without Edgible.

One playbook, seven steps →

Prefer to do it by hand? Install Edgible from edgible.com (opens in a new tab), then pick a card and follow its README.

Start here:

  • Get Edgible running: the first chapter of most playbooks.
  • Getting a machine: no spare computer? A virtual machine on yours, or a small cloud server.
  • Use your own domain: give any app an address on a domain you own.
  • Take it all down: remove what these playbooks set up, cleanly, keeping your data or not.
