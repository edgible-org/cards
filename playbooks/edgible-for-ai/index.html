Edgible for AI

Your own agent and a private model, used from your phone, on a budget. Two playbooks, one after the other: the second builds on the first. Every playbook begins with Get Edgible running.

The chapters:

  • Your own AI agent, from anywhere: your own agent on your machine, used from your phone, safely and with its costs under control.
    1. Get Edgible running
    2. Connect Claude to your Edgible
    3. Your AI gateway: the cost guardrails, first
    4. Your agent
    5. Run it from your phone
    6. A home for everything you've built
  • A private model for your agent: run an AI model on your own GPU computer and give your agent the choice of it, so private work never leaves your hardware. Builds on the agent playbook.
    1. Add a second machine
    2. Your private model
    3. Private in your gateway
    4. Private for your agent
    5. Give your agent your own files
    6. Choosing between them

On its own, with no Edgible needed to start:

  • A coding agent in a box: an open-source coding agent (OpenCode) in a virtual machine you can reset in seconds, with its own budget from your AI gateway.

New here? Start at all the playbooks, or go straight to Get Edgible running.
