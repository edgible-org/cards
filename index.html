Self-hosted setups you can copy or share

A card is a working self-hosted setup, made to be shared: plain files anyone can run on their own machine, change, and share back.

  • Made to shareNothing personal in it. You fill in your own.
  • The whole setupApps, ports, and who can sign in, published with TLS.
  • The same steps every timeCheck, start, publish, verify, tear down.
  • Proof it worksTested on a real device, with the date on every card.
